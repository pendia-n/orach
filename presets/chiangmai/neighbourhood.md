The dish is khao soi, egg noodles in a mild coconut curry crowned with crispy fried ones, pickled mustard greens and lime on the side, ฿50–80 a bowl. Khao Soi Khun Yai near Wat Lok Moli (late morning to early afternoon, sells out) and Huen Phen on Ratchamanka Road are the Old City's reference versions, and every local will name a third.\n\nRound out the table with sai ua, the herb-packed northern sausage, ฿30–40 a stick at any market grill, the chili dips nam prik noom and nam prik ong scooped up with sticky rice and pork crackling, and gaeng hang lay, the Burmese-style pork-belly curry. Tong Tem Toh in Nimman is the sit-down institution for all of it. Put your name down and wait on the soi. The Chiang Mai Gate market does the locals' breakfast, khao kha moo braised pork leg on rice for ฿40–60, from dawn. And this is the best city in Thailand to learn to cook it.
