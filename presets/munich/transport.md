The ticket can also be purchased at the airport by credit card and is already validated.\n\nWho it's for: For passengers who wish to travel from Munich Airport to the city centre and would like to make further journeys in the Zone M-5 area of validity on the same day.\n\n- More information: [www.mvv-muenchen.de](https://www.mvv-muenchen.de/en/tickets-abos-preise/single-ticket-1-2-3/ \"Website MVV – Airport City Day Ticket\")\n\n**Deutschlandticket:**\n\nWith the [Deutschlandticket](https://www.munich.travel/en/topics/lakes-mountains/deutschlandticket-excursions \"Excursions with the Deutschlandticket\") for 63 euros (in 2026), you can use all public transport in Munich. The best known are the [MVV app](https://www.mvv-muenchen.de/en/journey-planer/mobile-services/mvv-app/index.html \"Website MVV app\") and the [MVGO app](https://www.mvg.de/services/unsere-apps/mvgo.html# \"Website MVGO app\") from Münchner Verkehrsgesellschaft and Münchner Verkehrs- und Tarifverbund.
