If you take off for Thailand to escape the northern hemisphere winter, the time between late December and mid-April will give you a boost. February and the first half of March are the driest, with daytime highs of 36°C (95°F). Best for beach bums, conditions are sweltering during this time; lows can be as high as 28°C (82°F). However, these months are heavier on your pocket with hotel prices almost doubling.\n\nTo save money, visit in the shoulder season from late April to early June, when the crowds thin out. Weather-wise, this transitional period is hit or miss. While the rainy season starts in late June, it can be sunny for days. But heavy rains lash the island from September to mid-October, when the powerful monsoon brings moist warm air from the Indian Ocean. Stormy seas could prevent diving now, though windsurfing may be a joy, and accommodation prices plummet.
