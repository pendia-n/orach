537.74\n\n## Scooter and Motorbike Rental\n\nScooter rental is popular among travellers comfortable riding in mixed traffic, with standard 125cc models available from around 130 to 300 baht per day and larger maxi-scooters running 250 to 450 baht. Weekly rentals typically drop to 900 to 1,800 baht, and monthly rates fall between 2,000 and 4,000 baht for longer stays. Most rental shops include two helmets and basic medical insurance in the price, though always confirming coverage details before signing is worth the extra minute. Bicycles are generally not recommended given Phuket's hilly terrain and fast-moving traffic on the main coastal roads.\n\n## Ferries to Nearby Islands\n\nPhuket doubles as the main departure point for several of southern Thailand's most photographed island destinations, making a boat trip a natural add-on to any stay.\n\n### Koh Phi Phi\n\nRegular ferry service connects Phuket to Koh Phi Phi, with one-way tickets running roughly 430 to 620 baht depending on the operator and departure time. The crossing takes around 90 minutes to two hours, and Mu Ko Phi Phi National Park charges a separate entry fee of 400 baht for adults and 200 baht for children.\n\n### James Bond Island\n\nNo scheduled public ferry serves Phang Nga Bay's James Bond Island, so visits run through organised day tours departing mainly from the island's east-coast piers. Tours typically combine the island with sea cave canoeing through the bay's limestone karsts, and Ao Phang Nga National Park charges a 300 baht entry fee per person.\n\n### Similan Islands\n\nThe Similan Islands sit further offshore and require a full-day tour, with entry to Mu Ko Similan National Park priced at 500 baht for adults and 250 baht for children. The park closes seasonally during the monsoon months, so confirming current opening dates before booking a Similan trip is worth doing closer to the travel
