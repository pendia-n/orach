Located in the Horto region of the Botanical Garden (where most cyclists in Rio train daily), this modern coffee shop serves a roast blend of Yellow Catuaí and Yellow Bourbon from a farm in Patrocínio, Minas Gerais (north of Rio). The beans arrive weekly from Espírito Santo and are roasted by Mario Zardo (a former coffee producer).\n![rio de janeiro cafe coffee guide olympics sofa cafe bastarda kraft secreto curto specialty brazil sprudge](https://sprudge.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/Sprudge-RioCafeGuide-JulianaGanan-CurtoCafe_Customers_CiceroRodrigues_08-740x494.jpg)\n\nCustomers at Curto Cafe (Cicero Rodrigues)\n\nAt peak times (between 12 p.m.–2 p.m.) things can get crazy: huge lines will form in front of each [La Marzocco](http://global.lamarzocco.com/en/) (one for espressos only, the other for cappuccinos—their only two coffee options). Co-founder Gabriel Magalhães tells me that once a month, they’ve started promoting all-day events with the goal to familiarize customers with specialty coffee. The last one was focused on coffee bean quality and getting to know the coffee offered in the market, and they had 500 people participate. If that wasn’t enough to convince you to pay them a visit, how about this: Curto takes a no-price approach to their espresso drinks. They display their operational and material costs on a blackboard, and leave the pricing up to you—you pay what you think it was worth. I hope they won’t mind me saying I think it’s worth a lot.\n\nCurto Café is located at [Avenida Erasmos Braga 278, Quiosque 47 – Centro](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Curto+Caf%C3%A9/@-22.9047756,-43.1750253,19z/data=!4m5!3m4!1s0x997f5f8004f143:0xeed1dd70734f4c61!8m2!3d-22.9056162!4d-43.1754455). Secreto is conveniently close to Largo do Machado subway station, and less than a minute away from its beautiful church, Nossa Senhora da Glória. Gabriela Ribeiro, the founder, carefully prepares espresso drinks and other brew methods ([Hario V60](http://hario.jp/seihin/productgroup.php?group=VD), [Kalita](http://www.kalita-usa.com/shop-1/rkmij76zvo9btb9o88xgfkle32whgg), and [AeroPress](http://www.aerobie.com/product/aeropress/)), and sources the tasty food options such as sourdough bread, *pães de queijo*, cookies, and cakes.\n\nChances are you will also find, behind the counter, the coffee consultant Renato Gutierres, who used to work with [Coffee Lab](http://coffeelab.com.br/) and now adopted Rio as his home and base for his coffee training courses. Ribeiro and Gutierres are often making up new— and delicious—cold drinks made with coffee, in order to beat Rio’s heat.\n![rio de janeiro cafe coffee guide olympics sofa cafe bastarda kraft secreto curto specialty brazil sprudge](https://sprudge.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/Sprudge-RioCafeGuide-JulianaGanan-Cafe_Secreto_shelf_Detail_Serge-Pirodeau_and_Liana_Rangel_01-740x494.jpg)\n\nA shelf at Cafe Secreto (Serge Pirodeau / Liana Rangel)\n\nThe beans used at Secreto are from [Isso é Café](http://issoecafe.com/), but they sporadically have other roasters options in the filtered methods, such as [4 Beans Coffee Company](http://www.4beanscoffeeco.com.br/) and Coffee Lab. Don’t forget to try Secreto’s pistachio, chocolate, and salt cookie—a hit among regulars.\n\nCafé Secreto is located at [Casa 8, Rua Gago Coutinho 6 – Catete](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Caf%C3%A9+Secreto/@-22.9305227,-43.1799979,15z/data=!4m2!3m1!1s0x0:0x57b6dd4db7598ef2). It is located a block away from the Cardeal Arcoverde subway station, or, if you are coming through the beach, just make a turn on Rodolfo Dantas street—it will be on the next corner. Also, if you are feeling fancy, Sofá is located in the block right behind the legendary Copacabana Palace Hotel.\n\nThe beans used at Sofá come from the São Paulo roastery twice a month. The coffee options available, besides espresso, are Hario V60, syphon, French press, and [Chemex](http://www.chemexcoffeemaker.com/). They serve many breakfast-like food options and sandwiches that make for a quick and filling lunch.\n![rio de janeiro cafe coffee guide olympics sofa cafe bastarda kraft secreto curto specialty brazil sprudge](https://sprudge.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/Sprudge-RioCafeGuide-JulianaGanan-SofaCafe_Owner-CarmenUrurahy_PhotoCiceroRodrigues_01-740x494.jpg)\n\nSofa Cafe co-owner Carmen Ururahy (Cicero Rodrigues)\n\nOwners Anna Moreira, Carmen Ururahy, and Tania Campos are betting on cold brew, which sounds delicious in Rio’s hot summers. They created an extensive menu of cold drinks made either with cold brew or espresso, and whenever they can they take the chance to introduce Cariocas to these alternatives to hot coffee.\n\nSofá Café is located at [Avenida Nossa Sra de Copacabana, 300 – Copacabana](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Sof%C3%A1+Caf%C3%A9/@-22.966236,-43.1790834,21z/data=!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0x3530964e63585bf2!8m2!3d-22.9662413!4d-43.1790597?hl=en-US). Duncan Hay and his wife, Priscila Hay, run Kraft with a focus on brunch-like food options, healthy juices and snacks, and mindfully sourced coffee. The beans are roasted by Duncan Hay, who learned the craft from a friend in his native Australia. Unlike his friend, who has to wait for weeks for his coffee to arrive from Brazil—subject to import taxes and customs delays—Hay is just hours away from the source. He often gets coffee delivered straight from farms and co-ops in Minas Gerais to his roastery located in Cosme Velho, a Rio neighborhood right behind the famous Christ Statue. Duncan Hay is proud of serving flat whites with carefully sourced fresh milk as well (the source he won’t reveal).\n![Kraft Cafe owner Duncan Hay (Cicero Rodrigues)](https://sprudge.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/Sprudge-KraftCafe-JulianaGanan-KraftCafe_Owner-DuncanHay_CiceroRodrigues_01-740x494-740x494.jpg)\n\nKraft Cafe owner Duncan Hay (Cicero Rodrigues)\n\nKraft serves food and drinks all day long to neighborhood regulars, folks from the large expat community in Rio, and many beach-goers who happen to walk by on their way to or back from Posto 10. When in Ipanema, you now know where to get good coffee and delicious, healthy food.\n\nKraft Café is located at [Rua Aníbal de Mendonça, 55 – Ipanema](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Kraft+caf%C3%A9/@-22.9852248,-43.2124456,19.15z/data=!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0xb074e09a543107d9!8m2!3d-22.9846201!4d-43.2114923?hl=en-US). Located in the Horto region, it’s an accessible point for those who need to fill up before a ride, or those who just finished their daily rides in nearby Jardim Botânico Park. Its owner, Marcos Leta, is a diligent rider himself. Being an entrepreneur and founder of a popular Brazilian all-natural juice brand, he felt the lack of a place to get a good coffee in Rio, but where one could also feel comfortable enough to share personal stories and experiences—in his own words. Bastarda is a fraternizing hub close enough to riders they can always come whenever they feel they want to be in their own community.\n![bastarda do bem cafe coffee bicycle cycling retail rio de janeiro botanical garden brazil sprudge](https://sprudge.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/Sprudge-Bastarda-JulianaGanan-BastardaCafe_AlessandroAssis-Barista_CiceroRodrigues_01-740x494.jpg)\n\nSyphon bar at Bastarda (Cicero Rodrigues)\n\nThe syphon coffee and the matcha latte, made with Leta’s [Do Bem](http://dobem.com/) juice brand matcha tea, are a must try. They will be served to you in antique, unmatched cups sourced from Copacabana’s antique shops. If you are in the mood, there is also a good selection of craft beer available. To eat, Bastarda serves tasty waffles, tapioca, pasta salads, quick and healthy bites for riders, and an exceptional *pão de queijo*.\n\nBastarda is located at [Rua Von Martius 325 – Jardim Botânico](https://www.google.com/maps/place/R.+Von+Martius,+325+-+Jardim+Bot%C3%A2nico,+Rio+de+Janeiro+-+RJ,+22460-050,+Brazil/@-22.9646735,-43.2228299,21z/data=!4m5!3m4!1s0x9bd599d11fb53b:0x30783e4bef4f703a!8m2!3d-22.9646344!4d-43.2228443).
