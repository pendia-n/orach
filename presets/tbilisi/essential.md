In a true “monkey see, monkey do” fashion, follow the locals’ lead – purchase directly from wineries, supermarkets, or specialist wine shops. Altogether, we spent around 30 GEL ($11.5) on this bounty.\n\nOther popular souvenirs include tkemali sauce, Georgian tea, ceramics, cheeses (if you’re prepared to transport them), and, of course, Georgian wine and chacha.
